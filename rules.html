Mofu Cats Server Rules

Help us keep Mofu Cats cozy, safe, and pastel for everyone!

  • Respect Everyone: Treat all members with kindness. No harassment, bullying, or discrimination.
  • No NSFW or Inappropriate Content: Keep the server safe for all ages. No explicit images, messages, or discussions.
  • No Spamming or Flooding: Don’t spam messages, emojis, pings, or reactions excessively.
  • Follow Discord's Terms of Service: Breaking Discord’s TOS (e.g., illegal activities, hacking) will result in a ban.
  • No usage of rude and inappropriate words: Don't use rude or inappropriate words with members or staff.
  • English only server: Please only communicate in English so all can participate.
  • No Self-Promotion or Advertising: Unless allowed in a specific channel, no links, invites, or promotions.
  • This server is 13+. Anyone found to be under TOS age will be banned. We do not allow regressed users within chats, this does not mean we do not allow age/pet regressors in general.
  • No Impersonation or Fake Accounts: Do not pretend to be someone else, including staff members or other users.
  • Follow Staff Instructions: If a moderator gives you a warning, respect it. If you have issues, message a staff member privately.
  • No Stealing: Everything in server is p2u, comes with emotes to all gfx, layouts and everything.
