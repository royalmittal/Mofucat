Frequently Asked Questions

🐾 How can I join the Mofu Cats community?
Click Join Us in the navigation and follow the steps to join our Discord server.
🎂 Is there an age restriction?
Our community welcomes members 13 years and older. Please keep all content appropriate for all ages.
🎨 Can I share my own art?
Yes! We encourage all members to share and celebrate their creativity in our designated art channels.
🎁 Are events and giveaways hosted?
Yes, Mofu Cats regularly hosts giveaways and community events. Stay tuned in the events channel for updates.
📜 What are the server rules?
Our rules focus on respect, safety, and fun. Please read them carefully on the Rules page.
🛡️ Is the server safe?
Moderation is in place to keep Mofu Cats friendly and SFW. Harassment and inappropriate content are strictly prohibited.
🌸 How do I get roles?
Roles are assigned when you accept the rules and by participating in community activities. Check the roles section for details.
